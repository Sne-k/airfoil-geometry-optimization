reference_ltpt.csv: measured values for the Eppler 387 at R = 300 000, M = 0.09 (total pressure 15 psi), transcribed
from McGhee, R. J., Walker, B. S. & Millard, B. F. (1988). Experimental results for the Eppler 387 airfoil at low
Reynolds numbers in the Langley Low-Turbulence Pressure Tunnel. NASA TM-4062. Forces: table B1, runs 3, 4 and 5
(increasing angle of attack; lift and pitching moment from surface pressures, drag from wake surveys, wind-tunnel
corrections applied). Oil flow: table III, upper-surface laminar separation (LS) and turbulent reattachment (TR) in
x/c; at 7 to 8 deg the report gives natural transition instead (0.40, 0.30, 0.20), not listed here.
Drag uncertainty about 2 counts (appendix A). Test-section turbulence about 0.06 % at 15 psi.
